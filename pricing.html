BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Do you offer social media marketing?+

Yes. Content planning, graphics, captions, publishing and reporting are tailored to your needs. Your quote confirms platforms, post counts and community management.

Is advertising included in the website price?+

No. Social media, campaign management and ad spend are separate. You approve the budget and own your ad accounts.

What do the care plans cost?+

Essential care is ETB 10,000/month; Business care is ETB 20,000/month. See the plans above for update hours and included checks.

How do we work together across countries?+

We work remotely from the United States with businesses in Ethiopia and the diaspora, using email, WhatsApp and scheduled calls.

How much will my website cost?+

Starter is ETB 26,000 and Business is ETB 46,000. Your written quote confirms the scope, final total and any additional costs.

How long does a website take?+

Typically 1–2 weeks for Starter, 2–4 weeks for Business and 4–8+ weeks for custom work, once scope and content are ready. Feedback and integrations can affect timing.

Do I need a domain name?+

A custom domain is recommended. You own the account; registration and renewal are separate unless included in your quote.

Will it work on mobile phones?+

Yes. Responsive layouts are included and checked at phone, tablet and desktop sizes.

Can you redesign my existing website?+

Yes. Share your website address and goals so we can review the content, access and migration needs.

Can I update it myself?+

You can edit the HTML files. For editing without code, ask about a CMS; setup and ongoing costs are quoted separately.

What happens after launch?+

You receive the agreed files and handover. Your quote defines post-launch corrections; ongoing support is optional.

Is maintenance required?+

No. Choose a care plan, request individual updates or manage it yourself. Keep hosting and domain renewals current.

Can you build an online store?+

Yes, as a custom project. We agree on products, payments, fulfillment and hosting before development.

Who supplies the content?+

You provide approved text, your logo, licensed images and business details. Copywriting is available separately.

Can the website be in English and Amharic?+

Yes, as an additional scope. You provide or approve translations; both versions are reviewed.

How do I pay?+

Your quote sets the payment method, currency, stages and any conversion or cross-border fees.

Will I appear on Google?+

Basic SEO is included. Search engines determine indexing and rankings, so results cannot be guaranteed.

Can you help with hosting?+

Yes. We help choose suitable hosting. Domain, hosting and email costs are separate unless included.

Can I add features later?+

Yes. New pages, languages and integrations are quoted separately; some features need additional infrastructure.
