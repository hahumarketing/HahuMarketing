Privacy policy

About hahumarketing

hahumarketing is a U.S.-based website design and digital marketing business serving Ethiopia and the diaspora. Privacy questions: hahumarketinginfo@gmail.com or call +1 443-333-9976.

Information you share

We receive the details you provide: name, email, phone, business, service interests, budget, timeline and message. Do not send passwords, payment card details or sensitive personal information.

How we use information

We use inquiry details to respond, prepare quotes, manage projects and keep business records. Inquiries do not subscribe you to marketing emails. We do not sell this information or use it for targeted advertising.

Services that handle information

GitHub Pages hosts this website; Formspree processes inquiries and delivers them to our Gmail inbox. These services may process IP addresses and logs for operation, security and spam prevention. WhatsApp handles messages you send through it. Data may be processed in the United States or other countries where these providers operate. Privacy notices: GitHub, Formspree, Google and WhatsApp.

Cookies and browser storage

This site has no advertising pixels or visitor analytics. Project help temporarily stores your message in your browser session to carry it into the form. Downloaded briefs stay on your device. WhatsApp messages are sent only when you choose to send them. External services have their own privacy notices.

Sharing and retention

We share information with providers to handle inquiries and agreed work, or as required by law. Records are kept only as needed for inquiries, client relationships, accounting, legal obligations or disputes.

Your choices and requests

Email hahumarketinginfo@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion or an end to further contact. We may verify your identity and retain records needed for legal obligations or disputes. Rights depend on applicable law.

Updates to this notice

We update this notice when our practices change. The date above shows the latest revision.

Service terms

Working with hahumarketing

An inquiry is not a paid order. Work begins after both parties approve a written quote or service agreement and any agreed initial payment is received. The signed agreement takes precedence over these general terms.

Scope, schedule and approvals

Your quote sets deliverables, platforms, counts, responsibilities, milestones and exclusions. You supply accurate information, licensed content and timely feedback. Schedules depend on materials, access and approvals. Scope changes are estimated before work proceeds.

Pricing and payment

Starter is ETB 26,000; Business is ETB 46,000 for the listed scope. Advanced projects, branding and marketing are quoted individually. Essential care is ETB 10,000/month; Business care is ETB 20,000/month. Your agreement confirms totals, currency, payment stages, taxes and conversion or payment fees. Domains, hosting, premium tools and ad spend are separate unless included. This site does not collect payments.

Revisions and delivery

Starter includes one revision round; Business includes two. Each is one combined set of feedback within scope. Extra pages, features or a new direction are quoted separately. Your agreement defines pre-launch checks, corrections and handover.

Content, ownership and accounts

You own supplied materials and are responsible for their accuracy and permissions. After agreed fees are paid, we hand over the custom deliverables and rights specified in your agreement. Third-party licenses still apply. Keep domain, hosting and ad accounts in your name. We need your permission to share your project as client work.

Marketing campaigns

Campaigns follow an agreed plan and budget. You approve claims, offers and materials before publication. Ad spend is separate from service fees. Platforms control approvals, availability and distribution. Rankings, followers, leads and sales are not guaranteed; reporting follows the agreed measures.

Maintenance and third-party services

Optional care plans cover the listed tasks and hours. Your agreement sets scheduling, response arrangements, unused hours and cancellation notice. New features, campaigns and renewals are separate unless included. External providers have their own fees and terms; outages or policy changes may affect delivery.

Pausing or ending a project

Request pauses or cancellations in writing. Payments, refunds and handover follow your agreement and applicable law, considering completed work and approved commitments. Ongoing service changes are confirmed in writing.

Questions or concerns

Email project concerns to hahumarketinginfo@gmail.com. Your agreement covers additional dispute terms. These terms do not exclude rights protected by applicable law.
